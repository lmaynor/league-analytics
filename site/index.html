How This Works
  • Odds simulate the rest of the matchup week 10,000 times from live totals, rosters and the NBA schedule.
  • Swing is how much a category moves your win chance. Sweat flags the ones still up for grabs.
  • Luck is actual wins minus the wins your weekly totals would earn against every team.
  • Red Cheerios re-scores each week as if the starters ESPN had flagged Out had played.
  • Data is a snapshot from the last refresh.
