How This Works
  • Win chance. Each live matchup is played out 10,000 times from the current totals, rosters and remaining NBA games. The percentage is how often each side wins. Odds stay hidden until every starter on both teams has locked (each player locks at his first game of the week), so they can't shape a lineup.
  • Swing and Sweat. Swing is how much one category moves a matchup's win chance. Sweat flags the categories that are still close and matter most.
  • xWin. Every week, your category totals are compared with all nine other teams. xWin is how many matchups those totals would win, added up over the season.
  • Luck. Actual wins minus xWin. Plus means the schedule helped you, minus means it hurt.
  • Cat % and Power. Cat % is the share of categories won, with ties counting half. Power blends it: 60% season, 40% last four weeks.
  • Projections & Prediction Markets. Simulates the regular season 10,000 times from each team's category strength and remaining schedule to model title odds, playoff cutoffs, bottom-3 danger, and the race for last place.
  • Bench Regret. Swaps of a benched player for a starter he could have replaced, using their real stats for the week, that would have improved this week's result.
  • Red Cheerios. Re-scores each week as if every starter who sat out a game had played, so you can see the wins lost to injuries and rest.
  • Records and Superlatives. Single-week records skip All-Star breaks and two-week finals. Player awards are all-time bests, one per award, counting games spent in a starting slot.
  • Trade Machine. Simulates 9-category trade packages, modeling net weekly stat deltas and league rank shifts across all categories.
  • Everything is a snapshot from the last refresh.
